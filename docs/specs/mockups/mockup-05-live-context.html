─ WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN ───────────────────────────────────────────────

 STALE  A live row rendered without its update time.
        The pre-market zone is marked "frozen at open". These three need
        the opposite mark — still updating, with a last-updated stamp.

 WRONG  The frozen score silently recomputing when a live row moves.
        "Computed once at 08:00" and "responds to live rows" cannot both
        be true of one number. If the three are to move exposure, that is
        a recompute with a declared cadence, and the panel must say so.
