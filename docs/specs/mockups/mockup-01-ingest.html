D:\Dev\momentum\watchlists — drop, verify, commit

Priority 1

You scan in Deepvue, export the CSV with its metadata columns, and drop it in the folder with a dated screenshot of the filter settings. The system reads it — and checks it before it trusts it.

Margin notes

1Two hard errors only A malformed filename and missing provenance. Both refuse to ingest rather than guess.
2Age is shown, never enforced A watchlist that hasn't changed is a real state, not a fault. The flag informs; you decide.
3Git is the provenance record CSV and screenshot are both committed, so every universe you traded has a dated, immutable entry for free.
4The system never scans Your Deepvue scan defines the universe boundary. Nothing downstream widens it.
Open item Whether Deepvue can export the filter settings as data. Until it can, the dated screenshot carries provenance.
