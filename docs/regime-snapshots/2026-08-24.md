**Word of the day.**

**Implied correlation** — a number derived from option prices that says how much the market expects individual stocks to move together. Low readings mean the index can go nowhere while individual names travel a long way, which is the environment a stock-picker wants.

**Equal weight** — an index where every company counts the same regardless of size. Comparing an equal-weight version against the ordinary one tells you whether a move belongs to the whole market or to a handful of giants.
