**Word of the day.**

**Carry trade.** Borrowing money in a currency that pays almost nothing — historically the Japanese yen — and putting it into something that pays more. It works while things are calm and unwinds violently when they are not, which is why the Australian dollar against the yen sits on this card as a risk-appetite gauge rather than as a currency view.

**Realised volatility.** How much a price actually moved over a past window, as opposed to how much options say it is expected to move. Today the Nasdaq proxy's realised volatility is 22.8% a year against 11.1% for the equal-weighted S&P — the same market, two very different rides.
