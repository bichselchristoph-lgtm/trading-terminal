### Word of the day

**Backwardation.** When the nearest futures contract costs more than the ones further out. In volatility that is the stressed shape: the market is paying up for protection right now rather than later. Today's is the opposite shape.

**Duration.** How much a bond's price moves when interest rates move. Longer duration means bigger swings. It matters here because HYG is shorter duration than LQD, so a pure interest-rate move shifts the HYG/LQD ratio without any change in credit risk.
