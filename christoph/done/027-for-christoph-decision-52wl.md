# 027 — is `52wL` wanted?

## The question

**Task `041` ruled the basis of `52wL`. Your list of twenty levels does not contain it.**

Either the list is short by one, or **041 ruled a level the product does not have.**

## How it got here

**The design session invented three levels you never asked for:** `ORL5`, `ORL15` and `52wL`. Two
were removed. **`52wL` survived because a task had already ruled on it, which is the artifact
justifying itself.**

## What it would cost

**Nothing to add** — it is a standing level, a definition with no parameters, free to compute from the
same daily bars `52wH` already uses.

**One row on the rail**, and the rail is four lines for twenty levels, so the cost is a name in a
list.

## The argument against

**You listed `52wH` and `ATH` and stopped.** Both are levels you trade toward. **A 52-week low on a
momentum name is not a level you are working with — it is where the name was before it became
interesting.**

## My recommendation

**Leave it out.** Your list was deliberate and the omission looks deliberate. **`041`'s ruling then
records a basis for a level that is not rendered, which is harmless and honest** — better than adding
a row because a task mentioned it.

## To answer

no, ORL5`, `ORL15` and `52wL` `52wl are all intentional and to be rendered in the ui."
